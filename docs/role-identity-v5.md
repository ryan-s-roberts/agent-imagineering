# Evidence ontology v5: source-described role identities

The model classifies a source-described agent, landlord, tenant or contextually unique
contractor as a legitimate case-scoped party. `role_party` is a static entity kind, not a
fabricated named person. Its mention is anchored to the source role description. A personal
name, contact-system ID or imaginary CRM record is not required to use that party in a
communication, role, ownership or authorization record.

Mention detection, identity linking and assertion extraction all receive this interpretation.
The model selects links between repeated role descriptions when the continuing referent is
clear within the case/property. It keeps differing property roles, competing people or
unexplained label linkage uncertain. Explicit attribution may support linking a role-party
mention to a named person/organization; the host only materializes links the model emitted.
No host routine creates role entities after extraction, merges names by similarity, or
rewrites an identity gap into a correct annotation.

Missing personal details remain absent. A source-described role is an identity; a field
literally saying 'not specified' is not a party identity. Agency/system provenance identifies
an operational agency role without proving that a human sent or reviewed anything. Generic
contractor identity is usable only when the context establishes a referent; multiple jobs
or providers may leave a genuine ambiguity. A message about an unidentified repair can be
ambiguous about the task while its sender's identity is perfectly clear.

Role parties belong to the party domain/range. Source-supported role records specify their
role and context. Missing names/CRM IDs alone must not produce identity gaps or block an
otherwise supported proposed communication. Approval, work scope, task linkage, ownership,
timing and actual competing relationships remain independent questions requiring evidence.
Neither a role identity nor an instruction to seek approval supplies approval itself.

The intervention evaluates new model extraction and decisions on seven previously inspected
development prefixes:011,016,025,026,028,035,038. It is not a prompt-only frozen-evidence arm.
Original sources and cutoffs stay fixed; extracted packets may change. A separate012-at021
contrast inspects later label/task uncertainty and stays outside the seven-case denominator.
No cases041–050 are read. Independent invented examples teach role identity mechanics;
no target actions or test-case particulars appear in those examples.
